Approved Parsing Rules

  • Maximum 35 material lines per FMR.
  • No missing source fields are auto-filled.
  • Operational sheet is derived from the final -## suffix in LINE NO.
  • The source SHT field is not used.
  • Description beginning with PIPE → LF.
  • PIPET and all other descriptions → EA.
  • Historical activity remains source evidence only.
  • PDF matching is optional and handled as manual review.
